Design and Development of a Board Game with AI-Driven Players September 2025

Group project: Design and development of a board game based on a predefined set of rules, carried out as an introductory immersion project at the beginning of the academic year. My personal contribution focused exclusively on the design and implementation of the game’s artificial intelligence, particularly the decision-making algorithms for automated players. This involved translating game rules into logical conditions, defining strategic behaviors, and ensuring coherent and realistic player actions within the constraints of the system. Through this project, I strengthened my skills in algorithmic thinking, problem-solving, and logical reasoning, while gaining my first hands-on experience in developing intelligent behavior for software systems in a collaborative environment.
