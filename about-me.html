About Me

I am an international student currently enrolled in the IT & Network Engineering program at ENSISA. Before joining ENSISA, I completed two years of intensive preparatory classes in Morocco, specializing in Mathematics and Physics.

This academic background has helped me develop strong analytical skills, intellectual rigor, and the ability to adapt quickly to demanding environments. Today, I am eager to apply these foundations to the fields of information technology and computer networks, while continuously expanding my knowledge. I am a naturally curious person who enjoys learning new concepts, even when they are complex or challenging.

Sustainability & Civic Engagement

Beyond academics, I strongly believe in the importance of social responsibility and civic engagement. I participated in a first aid training session to acquire essential skills that can help save lives in emergency situations.

I also took part in a volunteer initiative organized by the local neighborhood association, aimed at gathering essential supplies for communities severely affected by a powerful earthquake that struck central Morocco. Many families in this region lost their homes and basic resources, making this experience deeply impactful and meaningful to me.

Currently, I am involved in an associative volunteer group under development within ENSISA, focused on promoting solidarity, mutual support, and community engagement among students.
